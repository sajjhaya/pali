CHANGES

Versions are numbered edition.revision. Newest first.

1.2 — 17 November 2026
  The books are retitled and have moved to github.com/sajjhaya/pali. The
  grammar is now "Pāli: A Reference Grammar" and the workbook "Pāli: A
  Workbook"; the files are sajjhaya-pali and sajjhaya-pali-workbook. The licence notice is rewritten
  in a shorter form, with the full legal code included, and the title page
  follows it. The terms are unchanged: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. No rule, form,
  example or analysis has changed.

1.1 — 24 October 2026
  A revision of the prose throughout both volumes. No rule, form, example or
  analysis has changed; the wording has.
  Habitual phrasings that read as formula rather than argument ("rather
  than", "which is why", "is doing the work", paired dashes, bold
  sentences) have been rewritten into plainer prose. Bold is now kept for
  terms and for the forms a worked example highlights. Prose em-dashes are
  fewer, and the grammar is about 3,500 words shorter, the workbook about
  4,000.
  Appendix B, the reading pathway, is expanded to seventy-two texts, with its
  figures (page, length, vocabulary coverage) measured from the corpus.
  Appendix D no longer carries production notes.

1.0 — 5 October 2026
  The first numbered version, and a pre-proof edition: the author's
  proof-read of the printed pages is still to come.
  The grammar and the workbook as two print PDFs, as markdown (one file per
  volume and one per chapter), and as a website with no JavaScript.
  Every printed copy now carries this version on its title page.
