PĀLI: A REFERENCE GRAMMAR AND WORKBOOK

A reference grammar of the language of the Pāli canon, with a workbook of
exercises and worked answers. It is written for new students and for
experienced readers, and it aims to make the grammar a way of reading the
Buddha's word more closely.

Every example is real canonical text, given three ways: the Pāli, a
word-by-word gloss, and an idiomatic English rendering. The grammar runs from
script and sandhi through nouns, verbs, compounds, participles and case
syntax to metre, and ends with appendices of paradigm tables, a reading
pathway, a note on the language of the canon, a bibliography, and an index of
every passage cited.

The text has been checked mechanically throughout, but the printed pages
have not yet had a final proof-read, so some corrections may follow.
Versions are numbered edition.revision: corrections raise the revision (1.1,
1.2), and a substantive change raises the edition (2.0). CHANGES.txt says
what changed in each version.

WHERE TO START
  Read the introduction and "How to Study" first. They explain how the
  book is arranged and give two ways through it. The workbook follows the
  chapters one for one.

CITATIONS
  A canonical text is cited by name and the page of the Pali Text Society
  edition, as in "MN 10 — M i 56". Appendix E lists every text cited and the
  page it opens on. Grammars are cited by section and page, as in
  "Oberlies §41, p. 178"; Appendix D names the edition.
