    Appendix E: Index of Passages Cited

    Every text this volume cites, in the order of the canon, with the page of the Pali Text Society’s edition on which it opens and the places in this book that cite it. A citation in the body points from the book to a passage. This list points the other way, from a text a reader is studying to what this book has to say about it.

    The page is the one the whole text opens on, read from the Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana edition’s own record of the PTS page breaks; for a Dhammapada verse it is the page of the verse. Where the column is empty the edition does not place the text on one page beyond doubt, for one of three reasons: its record gives a page but no volume, as for the whole of the Khuddakapāṭha; its two records of the page, the break marked in the text and the page noted at the text’s head, disagree; or it does not place the text at all. Chapters are given by number, the appendices by letter, and the two essays before Chapter 1 by name.
